# 90-second owner tour

The Owner Tour is a guided, skippable walkthrough for a professor, recruiter, or company owner. It demonstrates TraceGuard’s product story without authenticating a person, contacting a company system, changing a setting, or sending data anywhere.

## Story shown in the tour

| Step | Approx. time | What the visitor sees | Question answered |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 1. Security overview | 0–15 seconds | The priority queue, detection coverage, and synthetic-only data boundary | What does the workspace help a security team notice? |
| 2. Critical signal | 15–30 seconds | A synthetic critical alert and the linked account-takeover pattern | What needs human attention first? |
| 3. Evidence and decision | 30–50 seconds | Linked evidence, a human review path, and the safe containment model | Does the product make an unsafe automatic account decision? |
| 4. Access governance | 50–70 seconds | Default-deny policy, approved Owner/Lab Assistant roles, and the approval lifecycle | Who can use the workspace and how is access removed? |
| 5. Owner control center | 70–90 seconds | Launch gates, disconnected service status, escalation expectations, and a reversible pilot plan | What would be required before a company connects real systems? |

## Presentation guidance

Use the tour to introduce the project, then pause at any step to answer questions. The visitor can move forward, go back, or skip the walkthrough. The tour never creates an account, changes a case, approves access, configures SSO, starts monitoring, creates a backup, or contacts a person.

The small time estimate is a presentation target, not a countdown. A visitor can explore the full local, synthetic workspace at their own pace.
